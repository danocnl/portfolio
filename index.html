The Problem

      
Developers faced lengthy, error-prone checklists to make templates platform-compatible — flooding support teams with time-consuming tickets. Three focal problems were identified:

      
    
        
  • Users missing required steps in complex multi-stage processes
    • 
        
  • Disruption to preferred developer tooling workflows
    • 
        
  • Friction and failures during template platform uploads
    • 
      

    


    

      
      
    


    
"Allowing developers to continue using their preferred tooling was a great direction for both them and the business."


    

      
The Solution

      
A 7-step wizard for new templates and a checklist pattern for existing ones — both living inside VS Code. The wizard outputs boilerplate HTML/CSS/JS pre-configured with required libraries, metadata, and validation ready for platform upload. Steps cover workspace setup, IAB-standard sizing, advertiser login, placeholder mapping, and font selection.
